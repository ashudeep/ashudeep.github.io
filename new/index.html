About

I'm an AI researcher focused on making machine learning systems that work well for everyone — fair, safe, and aligned with human values. My work spans the full arc from theoretical foundations to systems serving hundreds of millions of users.

Most recently I was a Principal Applied Scientist at Microsoft AI, building agentic AI for search and developing safe, human-aligned multimodal LLMs. Before that, I was at Pinterest Labs where I pioneered bias evaluation frameworks and red-teaming methodologies for LLMs and image generation models in production.

I completed my PhD at Cornell University under Thorsten Joachims, establishing foundational work in fairness for ranking and recommender systems. I've also done research internships at Google Brain, Facebook Research, and Microsoft Research. I serve as Area Chair for NeurIPS and ICML, and co-taught the NeurIPS 2022 tutorial on responsible ML.
